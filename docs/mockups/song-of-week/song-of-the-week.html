How it behaves
  • Shows full at the top of Home.
  • Folds into the top bar when you scroll past the 3rd review, tap the arrow, or have already rated this week's song. The feed keeps your place; nothing jumps.
  • The fold is animated: the record shrinks and slides up next to Ask Beatie.
  • Tapping the small record scrolls to the top and opens the full view again.
  • The centre is a timer: green and full on Sunday, shrinking and turning red. The record spins only on the last day. In the last hour, only a thin red line is left.
  • No song this week: nothing shows, not even the small record.
