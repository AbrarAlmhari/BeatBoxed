About us

Music sounds better
when you talk about it.

BeatBoxed is a community where music lovers rate songs, write reviews, follow people with the same taste, and finally understand the lyrics behind the music.

What is BeatBoxed?

Not another streaming app. BeatBoxed is the place you go after the song ends, to share what it made you feel.

Rate and review

Give any song one to five stars, write what you think, and see how the community feels about it.

Discover together

Follow friends and people with your taste, find what's trending, and rate the Song of the Week with everyone.

Understand the lyrics

Ask Beatie, our AI assistant, to explain a line, translate a song, or tell you what it's really about.

Why we built it

We kept running into the same problem: streaming apps are great for listening, but there's nowhere good to talk about music. Reviews are scattered, lyrics in other languages are hard to follow, and finding people with your taste is mostly luck.

So we built the app we wanted: a home for honest reviews, real conversations about songs, and lyrics anyone can understand, in English, Arabic and beyond.
